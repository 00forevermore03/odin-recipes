Overnight Oats

If you want more delicious recipes, click here.

Description

Gooey, nutrient-dense spoonfuls of happiness and energy

Ingredients

  • Your favorite fruits. Chopped and or frozen
  • Greek Yogurt
  • Honey
  • Milk or Milk alternative
  • Thick-cut Oats
  • Empty, medium-sized portable container
  • Time. At least 8 hours of excess.

Steps

  1. Grab your empty container and fill the bottom layer with about an inch with oats
  2. Toss in a spoonful of greek yogurt for at least every quadrant. If tall like a mason jar, 8 spoonfuls should suit our needs
  3. Throw in your favorite fruits to top it off
  4. Squeeze honey drizzles on our contents
  5. Finalize with a cup of milk into our container.
  6. Cover up and place into the refrigerator for 8 hours.
  7. wait.
  8. Enjoy our filling, fruit-y snack
