Stir-Fry Ravioli

If you want more delicious recipes, click here.

Description

Ever bite into ravioli feeling like..

“ ECH!!! this is too soft!”

Well, then this ravioli recipe full of crunchy, beefy-goodness is for you!

Ingredients

  • Extra Virgin Olive Oil
  • Adobo All-Purpose Seasoning
  • Half pound of Ground Beef
  • Whatever your favorite marinara sauce is idk
  • One container of Three-Cheese Ravioli
  • Mushroom Ravioli
  • Parmesean Cheese
  • Cayenne Pepper
  • Skillet or Saucepan. Won't be eaten but consider it necesarry to cook these items.

Steps

  1. Oil up your skillet, no more than a teaspoon around the pan for lubrication.
  2. Drop your cute little raviolis into the saucepan of choice.
  3. Dress both sides of your ravioli with the adobo seasoning.
  4. Get toasting on the ravioli with 3 to 4 minutes on both sides. Medium heat
  5. Once a crispy brown, set our hardwork aside while we fetch our beef
  6. The pan shouldn't require much more olive oil but feel free to add some if it looks too dry
  7. Season our beef with adobo, and any other savory seasonings your impulsive mind conjures
  8. Beef takes longer to cook than our ravioli. Babysit it until the mass shrinks into smaller, crunchy pebbles
  9. When dark enough, return our ravioli into the skillet -- this time paired with marinara sauce
  10. Let our party in the pan cook itself up for around 5 minutes. Season your marinara to taste.
  11. Leave to cool for an extra five minutes, then enjoy!
